## F02 [HIGH] cryptographic-binding-failure

- rootCause: The violated invariant is enforced incompletely at the recorded root control. SpaceCommitCodec signs encodedContext(space, author, rev, ikm), excluding commitment. - remediation: Bind the exact commitment into the writer-authenticated signature (with domain separation) or explicitly treat the serving PDS as the sole integrity authority and stop describing the deniable MAC as writer authentication. Reject recovery envelopes whose signed binding does not cover the installed commitment.
- confidence: high
